# Legislative Document Codes

Reference table for the `bill_type` values seen in [congress_bills](../tables/congress_bills.md) (`hr`, `s`, `hjres`, `sjres`, `hconres`, `sconres`, `hres`, `sres`, etc.) and their citation formats. Full detail in [`../../Documentation.md`](../../Documentation.md).

| Document Type | Official Prefix | Common Abbreviation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| House Bill | H.R. | HR |
| House Joint Resolution | H.J. Res. | HJRES |
| House Concurrent Resolution | H. Con. Res. | HCONRES |
| House Resolution | H. Res. | HRES |
| Senate Bill | S. | S |
| Senate Joint Resolution | S.J. Res. | SJRES |
| Senate Concurrent Resolution | S. Con. Res. | SCONRES |
| Senate Resolution | S. Res. | SRES |

Citations typically prefix the Congress number, e.g. `116H.R.263` = H.R. 263 in the 116th Congress.
